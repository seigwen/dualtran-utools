# Privacy Policy — DualTran for uTools

DualTran translates text you explicitly select or type. It collects
**nothing**: no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no developer server.

- Translated text is sent to the service **you** selected: Google (default,
  free, no account) or, in AI mode, directly to your AI provider with your own
  API key.
- Settings and the API key are stored locally through `utools.dbStorage`.
- No other network requests are made.

Bundled engine: `@dualtran/core` (MPL-2.0), extracted from
https://github.com/seigwen/DualTran-extension.
